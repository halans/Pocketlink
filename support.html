Support

Last updated 16 November 2026

Pocketlink turns your contact details into a Lock Screen wallpaper with a QR code. Someone scans it with their camera to save your details or open your website. The app also has a How to use guide in Settings.

Get in touch

Found a bug, or have a question or idea? Email support@halans.dev. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you’re using, and what you expected to happen.

You can also open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so don’t include personal contact details there.

Make your wallpaper

  1. In Details, choose Contact card to share your contact details, or Website only to share a single link, such as your LinkedIn profile or Linktree page. A contact card only needs your name.
  2. In Background, pick a colour or gradient, or choose a photo. With a photo you can zoom, move and darken it so the card stays readable.
  3. Use Card position to move the card up or down, clear of the clock and widgets.
  4. Check the preview. Swipe the Edit card down, or tap it, to see the whole wallpaper. The clock button shows where the time and widgets will sit.
  5. Tap Save, then Save to Photos, or Share / Save to Files.

Set it as your wallpaper

  1. Open the Settings app, tap Wallpaper, then Add New Wallpaper.
  2. Tap Photos and choose the picture you saved.
  3. Keep the whole QR code and its white border visible, then tap Add.

Common questions

The code won’t scan

Show it from the Lock Screen, where app icons can’t cover it, and keep Lock Screen widgets clear of the code. Make sure the whole code and its white border are visible, and turn the brightness up when someone scans it.

I changed my details but the wallpaper didn’t update

A saved wallpaper is a picture, so it doesn’t update itself. After a change, save a new wallpaper and set it again. The same goes for Wallet passes: make a new pass.

The wallpaper doesn’t fit my iPhone

Pocketlink matches the iPhone you’re using. To make a wallpaper for a different model, choose it in Settings. On iPad, Pocketlink makes a 1080 × 1920 wallpaper unless you pick an iPhone model.

Can I add it to Apple Wallet?

Yes, with Wallet’s own Create a Pass, which needs iOS 27 on an iPhone with Apple Intelligence. Open Wallet, tap the Add button, choose Create a Pass, tap Photos and pick your saved wallpaper. Review what Wallet read, then tap Add. Pocketlink’s Apple Wallet help in Settings has tips on naming and styling your pass, and Apple explains the feature in Create passes in Wallet.

Where are my details stored?

Only on your iPhone. Your details and background are saved as you go, so they’re there next time. Reset all in Settings clears them, and deleting the app deletes them. Pocketlink has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

Who can see what’s in the code?

Anyone who scans it can read what it shares, so only include what you’re happy to give out. Website only mode shares just the link.
