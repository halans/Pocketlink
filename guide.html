How to Put a QR Code on Your iPhone Lock Screen

Last updated 17 November 2026

A QR code on your Lock Screen lets people save your contact details or open your link by pointing their camera at your phone. No typing, no business card. Pocketlink makes the wallpaper for you, free and entirely on your iPhone, in a few minutes.

What the QR code can share

Choose one of two modes in Details. Contact card shares your name, role, email, phone and website, and whoever scans it can save them as a contact in one tap. A contact card only needs your name. Website only shares a single link, such as your LinkedIn profile, your Linktree page or your portfolio.

Make your wallpaper

  1. Install Pocketlink from the App Store and open it.
  2. In Details, choose Contact card or Website only and fill in what you want to share.
  3. In Background, pick one of 14 gradients, a colour of your own, or a photo. With a photo you can zoom, move and darken it so the card stays readable.
  4. Use Card position to move the card up or down, clear of the clock and widgets. The clock button in the preview shows where the time and widgets will sit.
  5. Tap Save, then Save to Photos.

Pocketlink makes the wallpaper at your iPhone’s native resolution, so nothing is cropped or scaled.

Set it as your Lock Screen

  1. Open the Settings app, tap Wallpaper, then Add New Wallpaper.
  2. Tap Photos and choose the picture you saved.
  3. Keep the whole QR code and its white border visible, then tap Add.

Tips for a code that scans every time

  • Show it from the Lock Screen, where app icons can’t cover it.
  • Keep Lock Screen widgets clear of the code.
  • Make sure the whole code and its white border are visible.
  • Turn the brightness up when someone scans it.

Keep your code in Apple Wallet too

Wallet’s own Create a Pass turns your saved wallpaper into a pass with your name, icon and colour. It needs iOS 27 on an iPhone with Apple Intelligence. See the support page for the steps.

Changing your details later

A saved wallpaper is a picture, so it doesn’t update itself. After a change, save a new wallpaper and set it again.

Is it private?

Yes. Pocketlink has no account, ads, analytics or server, and your details stay on your iPhone. Anyone who scans the code can read what it shares, so only include what you’re happy to give out. Read the privacy policy.
