Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Pocketlink is built so that your details never leave your iPhone unless you choose to share them.

What Pocketlink collects

Nothing. Pocketlink has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no server. It doesn’t collect, sell or share any information about you.

What stays on your iPhone

The details you type and the background you choose are saved as one draft inside the app, so they’re there next time. That draft is encrypted by iOS, excluded from backups and never synced between devices. Deleting the app deletes it. Reset all in Settings clears it at any time.

Pocketlink also remembers your settings, such as appearance and wallpaper size, on your iPhone.

Photos

When you choose a background photo, iOS shows you its own picker, so Pocketlink only receives the photo you pick, not your library. Location and other metadata are not kept. Saving a wallpaper asks only for permission to add photos, never to read them.

What you share

The QR code contains exactly what you enter, and anyone who scans it can read that information. Website only mode shares just the link. When you use Share or Save to Files, the wallpaper goes to the destination you pick, which follows its own privacy terms. Saving to Photos may sync through your own iCloud settings.

Apple Wallet

Pocketlink doesn’t create Wallet passes. If you turn a saved wallpaper into a pass with Wallet’s Create a Pass, that’s Apple’s feature, covered by Apple’s privacy terms.

Children

Pocketlink doesn’t collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
